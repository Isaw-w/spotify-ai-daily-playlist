# My Spotify AI Daily Playlist taste profile

Write this in your own language. Precision matters more than genre labels.

## Foundations

- Three to eight works, artists, soundtracks, or musical traditions I genuinely love.

## Atmosphere

- How the music should feel and what activities it should accompany.

## Intellectual and aesthetic interests

- Stories, places, arts, ideas, or activities that can guide surprising recommendations.

## Avoid

- Styles, themes, production qualities, politics, novelty music, or emotional tones I do not want.

## Seasonal shelves

- Music that belongs only to a particular season or occasion.

## Daily shape

- Default: 13 tracks — 4 anchors, 6 bridges, and 3 bold discoveries.
